PLAN YOUR PROJECT ↗
01 — THE FOUNDATION

Better decisions begin before construction.

A well-considered plan helps establish the direction of a project. It brings together the intended use of a space, design priorities, site considerations and practical requirements.

ARCHBUILD's planning approach focuses on understanding the bigger picture while keeping the details in view. The aim is to create a more informed starting point for design and development.

IDEA · STRATEGY · DIRECTION
02 — WHAT WE CONSIDER

Seeing the project from every angle.

Each project has its own context. The planning conversation should reflect its purpose, site conditions, priorities and applicable requirements.

01

Space & Function

Understand how people will use the space and what the layout needs to support.

02

Site Context

Consider site conditions, orientation, access and surrounding context.

03

Design Direction

Establish a coherent design direction aligned with the project brief.

04

Practical Needs

Identify functional requirements, priorities and important project constraints.

05

Project Coordination

Consider how design decisions connect with the next stages of project delivery.

06

Future Potential

Think about flexibility, long-term use and the evolving needs of the space.

03 — A CONNECTED APPROACH

Design thinking meets practical planning.

Planning is not an isolated activity. It connects the initial vision with architectural design, interiors and the practical considerations of execution.

✦ A clear understanding of the project brief
✦ Considered layout and spatial decisions
✦ Alignment between design and requirements
✦ A more informed direction for next steps
04 — PROJECT JOURNEY

From the first conversation to a clear direction.

A structured sequence helps organise the early stages of a project and identify what needs attention.

Discovery

Discuss the vision, requirements, priorities and project context.

Assessment

Review available information, site conditions and practical considerations.

Direction

Develop a considered design and planning direction for discussion.

Next Steps

Identify further inputs, coordination needs and possible next stages.
